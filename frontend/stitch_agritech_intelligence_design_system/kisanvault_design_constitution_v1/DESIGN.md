# KisanVault Design Constitution v1 & Theme Card

### Core Philosophy: Farmer-First Simplicity & Calm
KisanVault is designed specifically for farmers. The visual language favors absolute clarity, large touch targets, natural agricultural warmth, instant comprehension (understood within 3 seconds), high contrast, and gentle earthy tones. It completely rejects cluttered SaaS conventions, dense metric cards, harsh high-tech neon treatments, and robotic AI styling.

### 1. Locked Palette & Atmosphere
- **Background / Canvas (`#FAF7F2`)**: Warm, breathable agricultural off-white / parchment that reduces glare outdoors under sunlight.
- **Surface Containers (`#FFFFFF`, `#F5F1E8`, `#ECE7DD`)**: Soft, organic layered cards with 12px to 16px corner radius and ultra-subtle 1px borders (`#E2DDD2`).
- **Primary Earth Green (`#244523`)**: Deep, warm, trustworthy foliage green. High legibility against off-white, used for primary actions and key anchors.
- **Secondary Sage / Leaf (`#556B48`)**: Warm moss/sage green with golden-leaf undertones.
- **Harvest Accent (`#705936` / `#F5E4C7`)**: Warm soil and grain tone used for highlights, seasonal status, and weather anchors.
- **Text & Contrast (`#20261E`)**: Deep charcoal-bark for maximum sunlight readability, paired with `#525B4F` for secondary labels.

### 2. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Body & Controls**: Humanist sans-serif for crystal-clear readability, open apertures, and large minimum touch tap targets (minimum 48px).
- **Headings & Warm Accents**: Restrained editorial serif (`Newsreader`) for section titles and friendly greeting moments, conveying heritage, care, and organic earthiness.
- **Type Scale**: Generous line heights, uncrowded labels, no microscopic microcopy.

### 3. Component Standards
- **Buttons**:
  - **Primary**: Solid deep green (`#244523`), white text, bold label, rounded pill / 12px curve, minimum height 48px.
  - **Secondary / Ghost**: Warm outlined border with subtle background tint, clear tap state.
- **Cards**:
  - 12–16px border-radius, clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F1E8` fill, 1px `#E2DDD2` border, soft gentle shadow (`0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`).
  - High information hierarchy: 1 primary message or metric per card.
- **Iconography**:
  - Simple, rounded, consistent 2px stroke agricultural symbols (sprout, sun, raindrop, soil, grain bag, shield, voice mic).
- **AI & Assistance**:
  - Warm, conversational, grounded. Represented with natural organic tags ("Kisan Sahayak / Farming Advisory"), warm sage pills, and immediate connection to real crop data. Never glowing sci-fi or robotic metaphors.
- **Data & Status**:
  - Plain language over cryptic technical graphs: "Good Soil Moisture", "Spray Safe Today", "Market Rate Up ₹40". Large, unmistakable status badges.
