## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a grounded, high-trust agricultural intelligence platform that unites regenerative agronomic wisdom with computational clarity. The brand personality is deeply rooted, scientific yet pastoral, warm, and authoritative. Designed for agronomists, crop consultants, farm managers, and agricultural operators, it provides actionable insights without sterile corporate chrome or synthetic technological tropes.

The visual direction blends **Organic Modernism** with **Editorial Clarity**. The interface dismisses cool, tech-blue gradients, stark clinical grays, and neon cyber accents in favor of warm golden-undertone forest greens, herbal sage, humus earth tones, and sunlit linen surfaces. High-level insights, executive summaries, and environmental syntheses possess quiet academic prestige through restrained serif headings, while mission-critical analytical metrics, controls, and telemetry readouts remain clean, accessible, and legible in high-glare field environments.

## Colors

The color palette shifts decisively away from cool blue-slate undertones to warm, organic botanical and earthen hues:

- **Primary (`#243E26`)**: A rich, warm cypress forest green with deep golden undertones. It provides grounding contrast, optical stability, and brand authority across key actions, active navigation states, and executive touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#5F7B46`)**: A warm herbal moss and olive leaf tone. Used for secondary actions, interactive toggles, contextual filters, and complementary data visualizations.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6239`)**: A rich loam soil hue, paired with **Harvest Amber (`#D4A373`)** for high-value telemetry callouts, advisory badges, and harvest cycle indicators.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#1E2721` & `#FBF9F5`)**: Text is rendered in deep peat neutral `#1E2721` over a warm agricultural off-white canvas `#FBF9F5`, preserving warmth without sacrificing readability or introducing eye strain.
- **Surfaces & Borders**: Cards utilize pure `#FFFFFF` with muted dividers (`#E5E0D8`), keeping data layers organized and breathable without clinical sterility.
- **Semantic States**: Ecological cues govern feedback—vital moss forest green (`#2E5528`) for healthy plots, warm golden amber (`#D48B38`) for moisture or stress warnings, and oxidized terracotta (`#A63A3A`) for urgent field alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes **Newsreader** with **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Newsreader**: A transitional editorial serif that infuses platform overviews, executive reports, narrative summaries, and field diagnostic headings with natural academic authority and literary warmth.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: A humanist geometric grotesque providing uncompromised legibility for all analytical interfaces, operational buttons, sensor tables, numerical readouts, and body text.
- **Field Readability**: Font scales maintain generous x-heights and open counters to preserve rapid scanning capability under direct outdoor sunlight and handheld mobile conditions. Numerical tabular figures are favored within sensor metrics and field telemetry logs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a responsive 12-column grid designed for agronomic maps, field cards, and dense telemetry data:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, 2rem outer margins, and 1.5rem gutters. Side navigation remains pinned at 280px or collapses into an icon rail to give maximum breathing room to crop telemetry canvases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 1.5rem margins and 1rem gutters. Metric panels reorganize into twin vertical columns; menus slide from off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout with 1rem outer canvas margins and 0.75rem gutters. Analytical cards stack vertically into seamless single-column feeds with touch-friendly swipe carousels for sensor ribbons.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Component padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (1rem) on mobile viewports and `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop dashboards. Internal metric groupings use `space-xs` (0.25rem) and `space-sm` (0.5rem) to maintain tight data correlation without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile, warm-tinted surface layers and delicate ambient shadows:

- **Canvas Base**: Resting background set in warm off-white `#FBF9F5`.
- **Card Surfaces**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by delicate warm borders (`1px solid #E5E0D8`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are tinted with forest-earth pigments rather than neutral charcoal or cold indigo:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 62, 38, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Hover & Focus Elevation**: Interactive panels lift softly on pointer engagement using `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(36, 62, 38, 0.09)` accompanied by a border shift to `#CEC7BC`.
- **Modals & Drawers**: Elevation overlays utilize soft diffusion (`0 16px 32px -6px rgba(30, 39, 33, 0.12)`) layered over a warm neutral backdrop veil (`rgba(30, 39, 33, 0.35)`) with a subtle 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects natural curvature inspired by seed contours, agricultural parcel lines, and smooth river stones:

- **Structural Cards & Panels**: Built with `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to maintain a soft, grounded containment structure.
- **Interactive Controls & Buttons**: Styled with `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) up to full-pill geometry (9999px) for tactile, handheld appeal.
- **Inputs, Chips & Micro-Badges**: Formed using standard `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) or pill shapes, creating clean geometric alignment alongside data tables and inspection cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid cypress green `#243E26` background with `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-xl` geometry, and `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. On hover, shifts to deeper olive forest `#1B301D`.
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined in `1.5px solid #5F7B46`, text `#243E26`, transparent fill. On hover, fills with warm herbal wash `#F1F5ED`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button**: Borderless, text `#5F7B46`, hover background `#F4F1EA`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Structural cards use `#FFFFFF` fill, 1rem border radius, and `1px solid #E5E0D8` outline.
- Diagnostic crop cards feature a 3px rounded top border accent colored by vegetation health status.
- Card footers and sub-headers are separated by subtle `1px solid #F4F1EA` dividers.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E0D8`, `0.5rem` border radius.
- Labels rendered in `label-sm` using `#576059`.
- Active focus displays a crisp `1.5px solid #243E26` stroke with a soft 3px halo in `rgba(95, 123, 70, 0.20)`.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- **Status Tags**: Full pill geometry with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding.
- **Healthy Crop Status**: Background `#EEF4EA`, text `#243E26`, border `#D2E2CA`.
- **Harvest / Soil Warning**: Background `#F9F3EA`, text `#8C6239`, border `#ECD9C4`.
- **Critical Alert**: Background `#FBEAEA`, text `#A63A3A`, border `#F0C4C4`.

### AI Intelligence & Advisory Modules
- **Tone**: Cooperative, grounded, and ecological rather than robotic or synthetic.
- **Advisory Pill**: Encased in a warm sand fill (`#FAF6F0`) with a harvest border (`1px solid #D4A373`) and `#8C6239` text, accompanied by an organic 4-point sprout icon.
- **Synthesis Card**: Styled with a soft parchment background (`#FDFBF7`) and a 3px left vertical indicator in `#D4A373`.

### Evidence & Citations
- **Inline Markers**: Compact numeric or leaf superscript tags (`#E5E0D8` fill, `#243E26` text, `rounded-sm`) beside automated recommendations.
- **Citation Anchors**: Positioned in card footers using `#F4F1EA` containers, displaying telemetry timestamps and soil benchmark references in `citation` type. Hyperlinks carry subtle underlines in `#5F7B46`.

### Iconography
- Consistent 1.75px monoline stroke with rounded joints.
- Metaphors reflect natural agricultural systems: unfurled foliage, soil stratigraphy, solar radiation, moisture drops, contour lines, and grain heads.
